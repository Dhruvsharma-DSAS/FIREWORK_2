Step by step
EnglishHinglish
1 · Pair the years. Use only overlap years where both sensors watched the same region and month.
2 · Ratio per year. Divide VIIRS cell-days by MODIS cell-days (skip MODIS = 0).
3 · Take the median. Robust to one strange year (breakdown point 50%).
4 · Shrink if data is thin. Pull toward the global k when few years exist.
5 · Convert. Pre-2012 MODIS × k = VIIRS-equivalent, with a bootstrap band.

Common doubts
Why not just use VIIRS?
VIIRS starts in 2012. Without conversion, 2000–2011 cannot be compared.
Is k the same everywhere?
No — it depends on fire size, land type and season. That is why k has region and month.
Does k fix clouds?
No. Clouds are handled by the clear-sky rate before calibration.
