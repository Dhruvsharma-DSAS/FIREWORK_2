Why a satellite misses an evening fire

A polar satellite crosses a place at nearly the same local time every day. MODIS on Aqua and VIIRS pass around 13:30. A field fire lit at 16:30 has no satellite overhead, so it never enters the hotspot record — even though the field burned.

Glossary

Cell-day — one grid cell on one day with ≥ 1 detection.

FRP — fire radiative power, MW.

Harmonisation — putting two sensors on one scale.

MAD — median absolute deviation, a robust spread.

Seam — the 2012 jump when VIIRS starts.

Most misunderstood

“Fewer hotspots = less burning.”
Not always: timing, clouds and sensor changes can hide burning.

“A hotspot is a fire boundary.”
No — it is the centre of a pixel that was hot at overpass time.

“p > 0.05 means no change.”
No — it means the test could not show one.
