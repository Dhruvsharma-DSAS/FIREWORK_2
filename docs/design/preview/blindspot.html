What can hide a fire
Overpass time
Polar satellites pass ~10:30 and ~13:30 local. Burning after 15:00 is not seen.
Clouds & fog
FireMask class 4 = cloud. Rate = fire / clear removes this bias.
Small / cool fires
Below the detection curve (FRP50). VIIRS sees more than MODIS.
Night & geometry
Different night sensitivity; large pixels at swath edge.
